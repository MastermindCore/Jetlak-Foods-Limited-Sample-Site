Who we are

An established food & beverage maker, built on what the customer gets out of it.

Jetlak Foods is a Kenyan food and beverage manufacturer specialising in bottled water, fruit juices, fruit-flavoured drinks, plant-based nutritional drinks and peanut butter. We take pride in producing consumables to the highest standard in the country — and even more pride in seeing our customers pleased and satisfied.

Quality, to us, isn't only what goes into a bottle or a jar. It's what a family gets out of it — generation after generation.

Common questions

The two we hear the most — from new parents and peanut butter purists alike.

Can my kids drink NuZiwa?

+

Yes — NuZiwa is safe for both children and adults. As always, it's best to check with your paediatrician before giving it to children under the age of one.

Is it normal to see a pool of oil on top of the peanut butter jar?

+

Completely normal. Grinding peanuts releases their natural oils, which are liquid at room temperature. As the jar sits, that oil rises and settles at the top — just give it a stir.

What sizes does Nuteez peanut butter come in?

+

Nuteez is available in 250g, 400g and 800g jars, so there's a size for a quick snack drawer or a full family pantry.

Where does Jetlak source its ingredients?

+

We work directly with smallholder farmer groups for our groundnuts, building a reliable and traceable supply chain rather than relying solely on middle-trader networks.
